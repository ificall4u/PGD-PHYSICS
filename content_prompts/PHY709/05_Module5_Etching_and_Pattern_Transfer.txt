Execute Module 5: Etching Processes (Subtractive Manufacturing).

Provide a ground-up, step-by-step masterclass covering:
1. What is Etching?: Removing exposed materials through chemical or physical means while photoresist stencils protect the rest.
2. Wet Chemical Etching:
   - Dipping wafers into liquid acid baths.
   - Isotropic etching (carving equally in all directions, causing undercutting under the stencil).
3. Dry Plasma Etching (Reactive Ion Etching - RIE):
   - Microscopic "sandblasting" with reactive gas plasma.
   - Anisotropic etching (carving straight vertical walls with zero undercut).
4. Selective Etching: Choosing chemical recipes that eat oxide rapidly while leaving pure silicon untouched.
