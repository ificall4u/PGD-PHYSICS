Execute Module 3: Photolithography & Pattern Transfer.

Provide a ground-up, step-by-step masterclass covering:
1. The Stencil-Painting Analogy: Explain photolithography as using light and liquid stencils to project microscopic patterns onto silicon.
2. Photoresist Chemistry:
   - Positive Photoresist: Exposed regions become soft and wash away.
   - Negative Photoresist: Exposed regions become tough and stay behind.
3. The Lithography Pipeline:
   - Spin coating photoresist evenly across the wafer.
   - Photomask alignment and UV light exposure.
   - Chemical developing to reveal pattern openings.
4. Optical Resolution Limits: Why blue/UV/EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) light is required to print lines smaller than a virus.
