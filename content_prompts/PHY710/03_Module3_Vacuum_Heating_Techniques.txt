Execute Module 3: Vacuum Heating Techniques.

Provide a ground-up, step-by-step masterclass covering:
1. Why Heat Material in a Vacuum?: Lowering boiling points and preventing chemical contamination (oxidation).
2. Resistive Heating: Passing high current through a boat or basket (filament) to melt source materials.
3. Induction Heating: Using high-frequency electromagnetic fields to heat conductive materials without contact.
4. Electron-Bombardment Heating (E-Beam): Firing a focused beam of high-energy electrons directly into a target crucible like a magnifying glass burning paper.
