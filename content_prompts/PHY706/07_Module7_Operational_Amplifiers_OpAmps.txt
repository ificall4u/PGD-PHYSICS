Execute Module 7: Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps) in Instrumentation.

Provide a ground-up, step-by-step masterclass covering:
1. The Op-Amp Concept: The "electric lever"—a tiny high-gain amplifier that boosts weak sensor signals.
2. Golden Rules of Ideal Op-Amps:
   - Rule 1: Zero current flows into the input terminals (infinite input resistance).
   - Rule 2: Negative feedback adjusts the output to keep both input terminal voltages equal.
3. Key Op-Amp Circuits:
   - Inverting and Non-Inverting Amplifiers.
   - Summing Amplifiers and Differential Amplifiers (subtracting noise from sensor signals).
   - Integrators and Differentiators for dynamic signal analysis.
